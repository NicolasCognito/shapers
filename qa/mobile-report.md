- Quick +0.5h and a 0.75h form entry updated totals and recent entries. GO! late Community notice returned `notice_late: true`, `state: unexcused`; “I’ll come” removed it.
- Impact refused a pause starting today and accepted the future two-week request. GO! submission without a reason was disabled. Admin direct pause set/removal worked; refreshed current-week badge appeared. Matic’s Community warning matched consecutive misses; Nina’s single miss did not trigger a warning.
- Attendance saved 20 records and the changed cell persisted. Light/dark layouts generally held up outside the listed defects. Settings tables supported contained horizontal scrolling; model dialog content scrolled with its footer accessible.
